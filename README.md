# Smart Cool Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Smart Cool provides aircon servicing, maintenance, repairs, and installation for homes and businesses across Singapore. The homepage redesign focuses on helping visitors find the right service, compare prices, and request an appointment.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make services easier to find.** The original homepage makes visitors work to identify the service they need. Dedicated service cards, clearer headings, and direct enquiry links give each service a clear starting point.
2. **Make pricing easier to compare and credentials easier to find.** The range of prices and technical options makes comparisons difficult. Structured tables and short explanations clarify the differences between normal servicing, chemical cleaning, and chemical overhaul. The company’s stated experience and number of homeowners served get more visible placement.
3. **Simplify mobile contact and booking.** Visitors previously had to look through multiple sections for contact and booking options. The redesign calls for a sticky mobile bar with Call and WhatsApp buttons and prominent links to the online appointment form.

## Design Improvements

Clear headings, consistent service cards, and aligned pricing tables make the page easier to scan. Direct enquiry links let visitors act from the service they are reviewing.
