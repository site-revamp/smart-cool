FAQs

Questions before you book?

Some quick guidance to help you choose the right next step.

What types of aircon services does Smart Cool provide?

Its listed services include normal aircon servicing, chemical cleaning, chemical overhaul, gas top-up, condenser cleaning, troubleshooting, repair and installation. Confirm the exact scope when enquiring.

How much does normal aircon servicing cost?

The published one-time normal servicing prices shown on this page range from S$40 for one unit to S$115 for six units. Confirm current rates and what is included directly with Smart Cool.

When should I consider chemical cleaning or an overhaul?

These are deeper cleaning options that may be suitable in some circumstances. The appropriate service depends on the condition and type of your air-conditioning system. Ask the technician to assess your unit before proceeding.

How do I arrange an appointment?

You can use the company's online appointment form or contact the team by phone or WhatsApp to discuss your service needs and preferred timing.

Does Smart Cool serve my area?

The company presents its service coverage as Singapore-wide. Contact Smart Cool with your location to confirm appointment availability.
